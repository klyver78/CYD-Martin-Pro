Star Ring

A space-game prototype for the Cheap Yellow Display. Your ship orbits a ring and always points at the centre, where enemies come flying out of the starfield towards you. Hold the middle of the screen to fire, and catch power-ups for better weapons.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (not mobile).

2. How to

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer and plug the CYD into USB.
  2. Click Install and choose the serial port (CH340/CP2102).
  3. When the install is done, enter your Wi-Fi. It's only used for updates.
  4. Tap the screen to start.

Updates: tap UPDATE on the title screen. The game checks this site and installs a newer version over Wi-Fi.

How to play

ControlsHold the left third of the screen to orbit counter-clockwise, the right third to orbit clockwise, and the middle to fire.
ShieldEnemies are shielded near the centre (inner dotted circle, drawn dark). You have to get under their path once they're out.
SaucerOrange. Flies straight out to its target point on the ring. 1 hit, 100 points.
SpinnerGreen, rotating. Spirals out to its target point. 2 hits, 250 points.
Power-upsS Spread (3 shots, 15 s) · L Laser beam (9 s) · G 8 Grenades with blast damage. Catch them by flying into them on the ring. Shoot one and it's gone.
Lives3. You lose one, and your power-up, when an enemy reaches the ring where your ship is.
